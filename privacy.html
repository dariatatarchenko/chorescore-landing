Overview

ChoreScore ("we", "us") is a household task-tracking app. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it. We're a small, independent team — we don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Information we collect

Account info: your email address and password (or the third-party sign-in you choose), stored and authenticated through our backend provider, Supabase.

Profile info: the name, avatar, and color you or someone in your household sets for you.

Household data: the chores, schedules, completions, and stats you and your household members create — this is shared with the other members of your household by design, since that's the point of the app.

Subscription status: whether you're on the free or Premium plan, handled through Apple's in-app purchase system — we don't receive or store your payment card details.

What we don't collect

We don't use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. We don't access your contacts, photos, or location. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 — see "Children's privacy" below.

How we use your information

To run the app: sync your tasks and household across your devices, send you the notifications you enable, and keep your account secure. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell it to anyone.

Who we share it with

Supabase, our backend/database provider, processes and stores your data on our behalf under its own security and privacy commitments. Other members of your household can see the profile and task data you share within that household — that visibility is core to how the app works, not something we can turn off per-field. We don't share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

Children's privacy

ChoreScore accounts are for adults and teens 13 or older — we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 as an account holder. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at the email below and we'll delete it.

However, the adult who creates a household can add profiles for other household members, including younger children, so chores can be assigned to them too. These child profiles aren't accounts: they don't have their own login, email, or password, and store only a name, avatar, and color chosen by the adult who set them up. That information is visible only within your household, stays under the account holder's control, and can be edited or removed from Settings at any time.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account, which permanently removes your profile and access to your household data. Some records tied to other household members' history (like a completed chore you were assigned) may remain as part of their household's records.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain uses of it. If you're in the EU or UK, these are your rights under GDPR, and you can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you're a California resident, similar rights apply under the CCPA — and to be clear, we don't sell your personal information, so there's nothing to opt out of on that front. You can exercise most of these directly in the app (editing your profile, deleting your account); for anything else, email us and we'll help.

International data transfers

Supabase, our data host, may process and store your data in a different country than the one you live in. By using ChoreScore, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in locations that don't have the same data protection laws as your own.

Security

We rely on Supabase's authentication and database security (encryption in transit, access controls) to protect your data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Cookies on our website

This policy also covers our marketing website. The website doesn't use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers of any kind — it's just static pages.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
