Support

Stuck on something, or just want to say hi? Email daria.tatarchenko.ux@gmail.com — it's a one-person team, so you're emailing the person who actually built the app, not a support queue.

Joining a household

Whoever sets up the household first gets an invite code, in Settings → Account. Share it with the rest of your household, and they enter it during sign up (or from Settings → Account → "Join a Different Household" if they already have an account).

Managing or cancelling Premium

Subscriptions are billed and managed through Apple, not inside the app. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ChoreScore. Cancelling stops the next renewal — you keep Premium until the current period ends.

Deleting your account

Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile and your access to your household's data.

Anything else

Bug, feature idea, something confusing — just email it over. Real person reads every message.
